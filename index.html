Privacy Policy

Xpose — Photo Editor  ·  Last updated: April 6, 2026

Version 1.0

1. Introduction

Xpose ("we", "our", or "the app") is a photo editing application developed by Hajdar Drljo. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Photos & Camera: We access your photo library and camera only when you explicitly grant permission. Photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers unless you use a Cloud AI feature.
  • Device Identifier: A random, anonymous device identifier is used solely for Cloud AI usage tracking and rate limiting. It is not linked to your personal identity.
  • Subscription Data: Purchase and subscription information is managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.
  • Usage Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics services. No personal data is tracked or shared.

3. Cloud AI Processing

Some advanced AI features (such as AI background generation, image expansion, and sky replacement) require cloud processing. When you use these features:

  • Your photo is temporarily uploaded to our secure processing server.
  • The image is processed and the result is returned to your device.
  • Uploaded images are automatically deleted immediately after processing. We do not store, save, or use your images for any other purpose.
  • No facial recognition data or biometric data is collected or stored on our servers.

4. On-Device Processing

The vast majority of features in Xpose — including filters, adjustments, beauty tools, background removal, drawing, and face detection — run entirely on your device using Apple's Core ML and Vision frameworks. Your photos never leave your device for these features.

5. Advertising

Xpose may display ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain data for ad personalization as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising).

6. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Apple StoreKit: For managing subscriptions and in-app purchases. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data. Cloud-processed images are deleted immediately after the result is returned. Anonymous device identifiers are used only for rate limiting and are not associated with personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Xpose is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Data Security

All communication between the app and our cloud servers uses HTTPS encryption. On-device data is protected by iOS sandboxing and device encryption.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Deny or revoke camera and photo library permissions at any time via iOS Settings.
  • Use the app without cloud features (all on-device features work offline).
  • Request deletion of any data associated with your device identifier.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
